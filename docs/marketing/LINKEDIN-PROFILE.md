# LinkedIn Profile Copy

Set these up before any outreach. Prospects check your profile within seconds of a
connection request, so the profile has to confirm you are the real thing. All copy
below is built around the core message (GTM playbook §0):

> **We build tuned agents for your stack of documents, processing them at high
> accuracy and linked into your systems.**

No em dashes anywhere (keep it human).

---

## Headline (220 chars max)

Pick one. The headline is the single most-read line on your profile.

**Option A (outcome-led, recommended):**
> I help construction & property-management teams stop hand-keying their paperwork.
> Tuned document agents, measured accuracy, plugged into the systems you already use.

**Option B (problem-led):**
> Still typing invoices, progress claims and leases into your accounting software?
> I build document agents that do it for you, at a measured accuracy.

**Option C (short):**
> Founder, Stackdocs. Tuned agents for your stack of documents: high accuracy,
> straight into your systems.

---

## About section

Three short blocks: the pain, the offer, the CTA. Write it in first person.

> Most construction and property-management teams still pay someone to retype the
> same documents every week. Subcontractor invoices, progress claims, lien waivers,
> COIs, leases, applications. The data already exists on the page; it just has to be
> keyed into QuickBooks, Procore, AppFolio or whatever you run on. It is slow, it is
> error-prone, and it does not scale.
>
> I build Stackdocs: a tuned extraction agent for your stack of documents. Instead of
> a generic OCR template, the agent is tuned to your actual documents and formats, so
> it handles line items and the messy real-world layouts the off-the-shelf tools
> choke on. You get a measured accuracy number on your own documents before you
> commit to anything, and you set the confidence threshold for what flows straight
> through versus what goes to a human for a quick check. Nothing uncertain slips
> through silently.
>
> I am running free accuracy audits this month for a few construction and PM teams.
> Send me a handful of documents you currently key by hand and I will send back a
> report showing exactly which fields we would auto-extract and at what accuracy. No
> cost, no account, no pitch. If you key documents by hand and want to see the number
> on yours, message me or comment "audit" on one of my posts.

## First-week posting plan (so the feed backs up the profile)

You are not posting for reach; you are posting so that when a prospect checks your
profile after a connection request, the feed confirms you know this problem. One post
before you start sending requests, then ~1/week. Full outlines are in GTM playbook
§17. The best first post is the teardown:

> "I ran 40 real subcontractor invoices through AI extraction. Here is exactly where
> it broke, and how I got it to 98%." Then: three concrete failure modes, what fixed
> each, the accuracy climb, and a soft CTA to DM "audit".
