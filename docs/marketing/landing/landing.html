How it works

A pilot workflow built around measurement.

The goal is not to show that a model can extract one perfect demo document. The goal is to understand what it can handle repeatedly, where it fails, and how much review is required.

Define the fields

Agree on the exact schema, required fields, formats, and destination systems before training starts.

Label real samples

Upload representative documents with expected outputs. Trestle splits training and held-out test data.

Measure the bundle

Each field is scored with deterministic rules, producing an accuracy report and confidence calibration.

Route by confidence

High-confidence results are delivered automatically. Low-confidence results go to review.

Proof artifact

The audit report is the sales artifact and the deployment gate.

A Trestle pilot ends with a shareable report: accuracy by field, representative failures, and the estimated review rate at different confidence thresholds.

Use cases

For teams where document intake is still operational drag.

Trestle is deliberately horizontal at the extraction layer. The work becomes specific at the schema, validation, and destination layers.

Finance operations

Invoices, statements, purchase orders

Extract vendors, invoice numbers, totals, tax, line items, due dates, and payment terms.

Compliance operations

Certificates, licences, policies

Extract holder names, expiry dates, policy numbers, insurers, and evidence status.

Intake operations

Forms and submission packs

Turn messy PDF packs into structured records and route incomplete cases to review.
